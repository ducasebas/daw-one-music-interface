# GUI 3.15.1 · SYNC: scope and validation

Release `2026.11.20-sync.1`, service-worker cache 64. Local candidate; publishing needs the owner's approval.

## Owner's Pi data (20 November 2026, read-only, no audio)

- **Last sync (10:33 UTC):** valid. Correlation 0.769, relative lag −5 ms, delay 0 ms, margin ±109 ms, screen delay 40 ms, meter trim −40 ms. Microphone input latency not calibrated.
- **Earlier attempts:** two failed at 10:07 and 10:09 UTC (exit 1); the reason was not logged, and the next run overwrote it. One was stopped at 10:10.

## Findings

- **The audio lag was already measured finely:** 5 ms steps on matched windows. On a synthetic test signal the old method found the lag within about 3 ms.
- **The ±109 ms came mainly from the screen-path term** (`displayUncertaintyMs`). The browser estimated it from 12 samples as (2nd-highest − 2nd-lowest)/2 + 16 ms, so one slow frame or poll inflated it. The rest was a fixed 15 ms plus capture buffering.

## Changes

| Request | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Log failures | `deploy/sync/capture.mjs` writes the reason to the journal (`Synchronization failed: …`) and keeps the last five attempts in `/var/lib/daw-one-sync/history.json` (outcome, time, reason or delay ± margin; no audio). The manager returns a sanitised `history`; the wizard shows "Earlier attempts". |
| More precision | **Screen path:** 24 samples (was 12); margin = half the interquartile range + half a frame, minimum 10 ms (`displayTimingFromAges`).<br>• **Lag:** after the energy match, the lag is refined on onsets (energy rises over 30 ms) in 2 ms steps. The fixed 15 ms is replaced by 5 ms plus half the measured peak width.<br>• **Sampling:** microphone envelope every 10 ms (was 20) and correlation sampled every 10 ms. |
| Start-up burst | The analysis skips the first second of the recording (was 0.5 s). |
