## Host-helper refresh

The Bluetooth codec, bitrate and stability come from the Bluetooth agent. The duration history and the new update page come from the updater and the device manager. These parts reach the Pi only through `sudo bash /opt/daw-one/deploy/update-appliance.sh --apply`. Without that step, the GUI shows "Not reported yet" for the codec and "usually a few minutes" for the duration.
