**Subject:** DAW TuneBox update — Louder HAT development sample?

Hi Andriy,

Thanks again for your help with the Louder HAT Plus and its standby behaviour back in October. I wanted to share some progress on DAW ONE, now called **DAW TuneBox**.

It is a music-focused Raspberry Pi interface for Music Assistant, designed around a 1280 × 400 touchscreen. It includes configurable skins and a separate simulator for testing standby, presence and fault handling without enabling real amplifier hardware.

The interface now runs on my Pi 3 B+ development setup, with real Music Assistant playback through Squeezelite and the Pi's headphone output. I have also tested grouped playback with my Volumio Rivo. These are early tests; performance, recovery and accurate synchronisation still need work. The Pi 4 remains the original design target.

Would you consider supporting the project with one **Louder Raspberry HAT Plus 1X development sample**, or recommending a more suitable current variant? This would let me develop and document the actual amplifier integration.

In return, I would share reproducible setup instructions, revision-specific integration notes, honest test results and useful issue reports. I would particularly like to investigate the standby limitation you explained: keeping the player available for multiroom while controlling amplifier power independently of a continuous audio stream.

I hope the resulting documentation and software could be useful to other hobbyists using your boards. Would this be something you would be interested in supporting?
